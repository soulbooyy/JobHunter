## Supplement: candidate source and projection proof

Scope revision **2026-09-24.S2M1S1-r1**. Provenance: CG03S1-BC1–BC3 and effective Q6–Q41; Q24 is superseded by Q38/Q41.

<a id="evo-027"></a>
**EVO-027.** Evidence extraction MUST have deterministic fidelity/identity/round-trip tests; Profile derivation MUST have source-scoped semantic support evidence, including citation-valid but unsupported upgrades, omitted abilities, excluded Header/contact/URL leakage and total candidate rejection. Evaluate actual protected Frames and one-request/zero-request-reuse behavior. DeepFit proof MUST distinguish retrieval miss, incomplete coverage/UNKNOWN and source-bounded negative, preserve frozen historical inputs and separate optional Preference Fit from Capability Fit. Optimization proof MUST use only the selected Resume, exclude other Resume/Memory/chat career facts, avoid fabricated quantities and show no formal mutation. These consumer criteria complement existing Eval boundaries; a schema-valid ref or model-authored explanation is not proof of correctness. Detailed future Fit/optimization eval protocols remain their consumed-scope work.

## Supplement: Entry-scoped incremental portrait proof

Scope revision **2026-09-24.S2M1S1-r2**; accepted CG03S1-Q42–Q46.

<a id="evo-028"></a>
**EVO-028.** Incremental proof MUST cover all six Entry kinds and structured-only Entries; body/background dirty detection; same-label Entries without cross-Entry aggregation; full current Entry context and no unchanged/deleted/other-Resume input leakage; explicit empty groups versus missing/duplicate/unrequested groups; cross-Entry refs rejected even when valid elsewhere in the same Resume; complete replacement, removal, order and exact-version rebinding. Prove reuse from a compatible same-Resume baseline across failed intermediate versions, latest-compatible exact-version reattachment (including repeated corrective full refreshes) versus new-version assembly, incompatible-rule full generation, mixed/zero-call provenance and baseline corruption rejection. Test ready/failed explicit FULL refresh, active AUTOMATIC supersession versus matching FULL dedup, receipt replay, source/default/ABA fences, crash before/after dispatch and bounded deterministic recovery of the frozen merge without redispatch. Evaluate actual Frames and Entry-local semantic support/omission; structural coverage of output groups alone does not prove model quality. Verify one request for all dirty Entries in the current consumer version and fail-closed capacity with no hidden truncation/batching/retry. No requirement mandates identical model wording across full and incremental runs; evidence must establish supported Entry scope and correct retained/replaced groups.
