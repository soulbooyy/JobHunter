> **Current applicability — 2026-09-24.S2M1S1-r1.** Existing Eval ownership/trial integrity survives; EVO-027 extends the required consumer proof, not a runtime model judge. The new requirements below are the normative replacement for that scope. Earlier text/IDs remain historical provenance, not a legacy implementation requirement. [Accepted decisions](../../design/contract/sl-02-m1-supplement-grill.md); [current review](../../progress/traceability.md#67-sl-02m1-supplement-reviewed-scope).

The **2026-09-24.S3M2-r1** extension specifies isolation, retained evidence, evaluator independence and export boundaries, not executed acceptance. EVO-022–026 close the concrete exercise agreement; task-specific product semantic quality and executable proof remain separate; see [M2 readiness](../../progress/traceability.md#66-sl-03m2-reviewed-scope-and-interface-evidence).

<a id="evo-001"></a>
**EVO-001.** **Real component evidence.** M1 acceptance MUST exercise the actual Runtime and persistence components with controlled adapters, temporary stores and deterministic fault injection. Pure mocks of the Runtime are insufficient. Controlled data-only formats require golden logical-object→exact UTF-8/length/hash fixtures covering their actual Unicode, number, key-order and escaping rules. A fixture format is not the production Provider schema. Count actual adapter calls and prove no hidden retry/fallback within this boundary; real production SDK verification is M2 work.

Decision sources: CG05-Q1, CG05-Q56, CG05-Q77, CG05-Q88, CG05-Q90, CG05-Q116.

<a id="evo-002"></a>
**EVO-002.** **Authority and uncertain dispatch proof.** Exercise first grant, revoke without increment, regrant, overflow refusal, stale revoke and competing grants. Prove an original live winner with confirmed intent commit and no adapter entry may continue after acknowledgement uncertainty, while crash/restart, copied context, another path or lost proof cannot reuse that intent. Include grant acknowledgement uncertainty: matching persisted owner/generation alone cannot authorize a second path. Assert stale/new generations cannot first-publish the original generation's response, and PREPARED proven undispatched recovery uses fresh current authority.

Decision sources: CG05-Q5, CG05-Q15, CG05-Q27, CG05-Q28, CG05-Q31, CG05-Q66, CG05-Q77, CG05-Q85, CG05-Q112, CG05-Q113, CG05-Q126.

<a id="evo-003"></a>
**EVO-003.** **Response publication and byte proof.** Inject faults around response receipt, atomic persistence, acknowledgement and local processing. Prove one immutable response, complete-byte verification, deterministic format rejection, Invocation-derived format authority and exactly the original four-field publication result on confirmation. Verify same-byte confirmation after ending/revocation/deadline, different-byte conflict, and corruption/absent-reader distinctions without confirmation-side writes. Exercise competing identical/different publishers and uncertain commits against real durable state; never repair by another remote request.

Decision sources: CG05-Q8, CG05-Q10, CG05-Q19, CG05-Q39, CG05-Q45, CG05-Q46, CG05-Q56, CG05-Q89, CG05-Q91, CG05-Q92, CG05-Q93, CG05-Q94, CG05-Q118, CG05-Q125, CG05-Q130.

<a id="evo-004"></a>
**EVO-004.** **Ending and recovery proof.** Exercise cancellation/response races, first-ending arbitration, whole-Run consumer completion, sibling fencing, ownerless coordination versus concurrent claim and already-committed business results without unnecessary response access. Distinguish permitted bounded postdeadline local recovery from forbidden new remote dispatch/late first publication. Inject missing/corrupt payload, missing reader/consumer, format-invalid bytes, storage unreadability and failed ending persistence; assert correct convergence or honest unresolved outcome, no replay, no unrelated application failure and no fictitious terminal state. Include OPEN with no Invocation and consumer UNRESOLVED/DENY mappings; missing denial mapping must prevent enabling that recovery path.

Decision sources: CG05-Q30, CG05-Q35, CG05-Q36, CG05-Q58, CG05-Q62, CG05-Q63, CG05-Q64, CG05-Q69, CG05-Q71, CG05-Q72, CG05-Q73, CG05-Q74, CG05-Q82, CG05-Q83, CG05-Q95, CG05-Q104, CG05-Q105, CG05-Q111, CG05-Q127, CG05-Q128.

<a id="evo-005"></a>
**EVO-005.** **Size and incomplete-remote evidence.** Show full serialized-envelope size accounting at the captured limit. Confirmed complete remote termination plus oversized serialized response must produce atomic FAILED/RESPONSE_TOO_LARGE and rejection evidence without a body. An early buffer cutoff before terminal evidence must preserve remote uncertainty and null rejection marker. Cancellation or another ending that won first must remain unchanged. No unbounded draining, truncated durability, replay or zero-usage assumption may be used to pass these cases. Bounded reception is permitted; obtaining terminal evidence must not require continuing beyond resource limits.

Decision sources: CG05-Q48, CG05-Q68, CG05-Q76, CG05-Q87, CG05-Q99, CG05-Q101, CG05-Q102.

<a id="evo-006"></a>
**EVO-006.** **Controlled Tool proof.** Instantiate one typed controlled exact-version local-read action agreement with exact inputs, current permission, completion/result evidence, required dependency failures and replay conditions. Prove valid result reuse, safe unfinished same-Invocation re-execution under fresh qualification and immutable inputs, and refusal for missing permission/required sources or unknown action recovery. Prove the action performs no hidden Ensure, remote parse or business write, and lineage-only refs do not become extra payload dependencies. No production Tool catalog or universal side-effect mechanism is required.

Decision sources: CG05-Q70, CG05-Q116, CG05-Q121, CG05-Q122, CG05-Q123, CG05-Q124.

<a id="evo-007"></a>
**EVO-007.** **Storage and evidence accounting.** Prove forward migration from the actual preceding head preserves prior consumer data/receipts and introduces no historical Run/Invocation backfill. Test OPEN payload protection, terminal retention in M1, ownership recovery, rollback/commit uncertainty and wall-clock rollback without fabricated ordering. Run maintained applicable prior-consumer regression checks. Record requirement→code/test mappings and actual commands/outcomes separately from Contract review; normative publication alone MUST NOT claim executed migrations, Runtime acceptance, semantic capability or parent-Slice completion.

Decision sources: CG05-Q49, CG05-Q51, CG05-Q80, CG05-Q88, CG05-Q119.


## SL-03.M2 protected semantic scope

> Normative scope revision: **2026-09-24.S3M2-r1**. English is authoritative. This defines required behavior, not implemented or executed acceptance. Earlier scoped consumers remain unchanged.

[Decision register](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md) · [Contract index](../index.md)

These M2 requirements extend EVO-001–007 without claiming semantic business quality, installed LangGraph/Langfuse or production execution. The controlling scope is CG06, including its consumer deferrals and conformance-only exercise.

<a id="evo-008"></a>
**EVO-008.** **Real isolated paths.** Each trial_id is an independent UUIDv4, distinct from Run/platform trace IDs, without a Domain Eval aggregate. Execute the real Application/Domain/Repository/Harness/Gateway/adapter path in an isolated test Workspace reconstructed from declared fixtures. Real commits affect only that environment. Replay replaces the controlled external transport, not the Agent/admission logic; unit mocks must disclose their narrower claim. Trials do not inherit previous mutations, permissions, Memory or production defaults. Evaluation-only reference answers, control events, evaluator rubrics and unreached future inputs must not leak into Agent inputs; explicitly admitted fixture task/source data remains available through its owned Context/Tool path.

Decision sources: [CG06-Q1](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q1), [CG06-Q145](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q145), [CG06-Q146](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q146), [CG06-Q156](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q156), [CG06-Q158](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q158), [CG06-Q215](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q215).

<a id="evo-009"></a>
**EVO-009.** **Versioned exact fixtures.** Fixture semantics MUST have explicit format/version, exact reconstructible inputs/identities/configuration and deterministic hydration validation. Missing/incompatible required state rejects the Trial; no latest fallback. JSON bundle plus controlled hydrator is the initial representation, not a perpetual exchange format. Retain exact dataset/configuration/runner/protocol references and mark replay/live mode. Replay must use controlled transport with exact semantic request matching, allowing only declared irrelevant transport differences; it cannot fall back live. Live uses only the Trial's explicitly allowed reviewed Provider configuration; current M2 permits reviewed official DeepSeek configurations, not inherited production connections.

Decision sources: [CG06-Q39](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q39), [CG06-Q46](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q46), [CG06-Q137](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q137), [CG06-Q147](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q147), [CG06-Q157](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q157), [CG06-Q158](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q158).

<a id="evo-010"></a>
**EVO-010.** **Independent outcomes and checks.** Freeze the expected required/optional check set and applicability before observing results. Each check conclusion is PASS, FAIL, INCOMPLETE, ERROR, UNASSESSABLE or NOT_APPLICABLE with its protocol-owned reason/evidence. Correct rejection can pass a case; missing evidence cannot become PASS or disappear by removing a check. Task outcome, check findings, evidence availability and export delivery remain separate. A malformed evaluator response is not successful judgment. Replay/synthetic usage and ledger values must be labeled as such, never reported as newly incurred live-model cost.

Decision sources: [CG06-Q148](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q148), [CG06-Q152](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q152), [CG06-Q153](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q153), [CG06-Q156](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q156), [CG06-Q160](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q160).

<a id="evo-011"></a>
**EVO-011.** **Check and attempt identity.** Every actual evaluator execution receives evaluator_attempt_id UUIDv4 before execution. Retain its trial_id, check_key, exact evaluator_config_ref, admitted evidence manifest/reference, conclusion and reason_code; bounded explanation and protocol-defined metrics are optional and cannot replace evidence. A completed result has those fields; unfinished attempt evidence must not pretend to have a conclusion. Repeated evaluation is a distinct attempt, not overwrite/best-of selection or implicit model retry. Retain each attempt's actual result/error and independently associated resource/usage/cost evidence, preserving unknowns and the exact applicable configuration/basis. Evaluator work MUST NOT debit the tested Agent's foreground Budget. This requires no universal evaluator envelope or business Runtime dispatch guarantee; no Universal Attempt lifecycle is inferred.

Decision sources: [CG06-Q145](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q145), [CG06-Q153](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q153), [CG06-Q155](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q155), [CG06-Q159](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q159), [CG06-Q161](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q161).

<a id="evo-012"></a>
**EVO-012.** **Conditional LLM evaluator.** M2 infrastructure MUST support the isolation and evidence boundaries for independent evaluator invocations with their own controlled configuration/resources/evidence admission, but MUST NOT deliver a mandatory Generic Quality Judge. When a real consumer enables an LLM evaluator, its first protocol is a bounded read-only invocation of controlled rubric and admitted retained evidence: no Tools, Memory, external retrieval or business writes. It inherits neither the Agent's permission nor its Runtime reservation/recovery guarantees. The actual consumer owns rubric and PASS/FAIL meaning; a judge never owns business success. Secrets/production connections are not inherited implicitly.

Decision sources: [CG06-Q149](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q149), [CG06-Q154](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q154), [CG06-Q155](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q155), [CG06-Q158](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q158).

<a id="evo-013"></a>
**EVO-013.** **Evidence manifest.** Retain the original Trial's immutable evidence manifest: canonical exact references plus format/hash/integrity identity when availability is assured; otherwise controlled Eval-owned evidence copies only where necessary. Retain actual post-state evidence for mutable observations, not just a live pointer. Required capture failure makes affected checks incomplete/error according to the check protocol without rolling back a task or rerunning it. Missing optional spans do not invalidate a check with sufficient local evidence. Full Runtime payload copies are not a default second archive.

Decision sources: [CG06-Q47](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q47), [CG06-Q50](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q50), [CG06-Q149](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q149), [CG06-Q150](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q150), [CG06-Q151](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q151), [CG06-Q160](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q160).

<a id="evo-014"></a>
**EVO-014.** **Re-evaluation.** Re-evaluation consumes retained evidence from the original Trial, with independently identified evaluator execution and exact new evaluator/rubric/check configuration. It MUST NOT rerun the Agent, rehydrate then execute to guess the original post-state, overwrite prior findings or silently choose the best result. If required evidence is unavailable, preserve that limitation. Later Budget facts may support a new explicitly scoped assessment, not rewrite what an earlier attempt knew.

Decision sources: [CG06-Q150](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q150), [CG06-Q151](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q151), [CG06-Q152](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q152), [CG06-Q153](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q153), [CG06-Q155](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q155), [CG06-Q159](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q159), [CG06-Q161](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q161).

<a id="evo-015"></a>
**EVO-015.** **Pre-export admission.** Callback and explicit observation paths MUST share a pre-export allowlist. Default admitted classes are necessary opaque IDs, exact protocol/configuration keys and versions, hashes, source categories, finite counts/usage, exact CNY accounting values with certainty, bounded timings and typed outcomes. Secrets, raw task/Frame/response/reasoning/Tool payloads, arbitrary error strings and business data are excluded. Self-hosting does not expand permission. Apply admission before SDK processing that can upload media or log/export raw content; a late masking callback alone is insufficient. Raw evaluator evidence has its own admission and is not automatically telemetry.

Decision sources: [CG06-Q89](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q89), [CG06-Q94](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q94), [CG06-Q110](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q110), [CG06-Q149](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q149), [CG06-Q150](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q150), [CG06-Q154](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q154), [CG06-Q158](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q158), [CG06-Q213](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q213), [CG06-Q214](../../design/contract/sl-03-m2-grill.md#cg06-q214).

<a id="evo-016"></a>
**EVO-016.** **Correlation and count ownership.** Explicit Harness observations derived from canonical Invocation/Budget evidence own countable model/tool identity and usage/cost projections. LangGraph callbacks describe workflow mechanics and correlate to those identities without duplicating a generation or cost. Native platform pricing/inferred usage never becomes Budget authority; do not mislabel exact CNY as USD/float expense or manufacture zero for unknown cost. Late accounting retains original invocation_id and does not change original execution timing/outcome. An admitted correlated non-generation accounting event may represent it after the execution observation ends; do not require ended-span mutation or hold execution spans open until settlement. Prior Trial findings remain immutable.
