## 1. Reading and evidence baseline

[Progress recording rules](README.md) owns traceability/status discipline. The [Decision Register](../design/grill-me-design-tree.md) and its detailed modules preserve provenance; the [English authoring coverage index](../../.scratch/document-authoring-spec.en.md#d-decision-coverage-index) supplies the complete existing-record index. Later scoped corrections control even when an earlier record remains ACCEPTED. The short topic phrases below are locators for effective clauses and excluded historical readings, not replacement requirement prose.

P links identify Product behavior/scope; A links identify Architecture mechanisms or Contract responsibility planning; D links identify Development process obligations. V links identify required proof or an explicit exclusion/pending-detail section in Acceptance. These roles explain why one row can link several documents without assigning the same fact to competing authorities. The [W5 handoff](../../.scratch/w5-progress-handoff.md) preserves original scoped review evidence and now has a revised W6 planning entry; neither is product-test evidence. The [user-approved process revision](../../.scratch/document-authoring-spec.en.md#f-user-approved-delivery-process-revision) adds independent Implementation Plan as the seventh category, assigns W6 planning/W7 joint review and replaces whole-system Contract ordering with progressive normative scope. The 2026-09-19 CS5 refinement retains twelve macro Slices and allows internal milestones to independently complete required Contracts and enter development; other milestones in the same Slice may remain pending. Parent completion requires all required milestones and integrated proof. Its authority is the later user instruction, not a new Q/S record.
